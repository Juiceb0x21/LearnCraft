About Us

At LearnCraft, we connect English, technology, and game-based learning to create high-impact digital educational experiences across Chile.

We Are LearnCraft

We are a team committed to transforming education in Chile through game-based learning, creativity, and digital tools with real impact in the classroom.

LearnCraft is developed with the support of U.S. Funding and within the America 250 Celebration framework, promoting initiatives that strengthen innovation, international collaboration, and access to meaningful digital learning opportunities.

Our purpose is to promote the development of the country through game-based learning, strengthening creativity, critical thinking, collaboration, and the professional use of digital tools inside the school environment. We support schools through a scalable model that allows them to experience the methodology and later replicate it within their own educational community.

Services

We use Minecraft Education as a pedagogical tool to digitalize learning and develop academic, social, and digital skills in real classroom contexts.

Minecraft Education Licenses and Classroom Implementation

Schools receive annual Minecraft Education licenses so teachers and students can work with structured game-based learning experiences that strengthen communication, creativity, and problem-solving.

Teacher Training and Official Resources

We provide teacher training in Minecraft Education together with the official workbook for educators, helping schools integrate English, technology, and curriculum goals into practical and motivating projects.

Institutional Recognition and National Opportunities

Participating schools can access institutional certification for their commitment to educational innovation, regional workshops and webinars, and opportunities for teachers to join major in-person EdTech events in Santiago.
